\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

Programming languages differ widely in how much energy the same task
needs~\cite{pereira2017energy}, and energy labels for languages have been
tried with practitioners~\cite{pereira2024labels}.
Training large models has a carbon cost that is easy to
overlook~\cite{strubell2019energy,patterson2021carbon}, and calculators such as
Green Algorithms estimate it from the hardware and the
runtime~\cite{lannelongue2021green}.
Schwartz et al.\ ask researchers to report efficiency next to
accuracy~\cite{schwartz2020green}.
