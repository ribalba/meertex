# The narration of the demo video, one scene per [section] and one sentence per line.
#
# make_video.py speaks every sentence on its own, so the subtitles and the cuts follow
# the sentences. The section names are the scene ids of make_video.py, and the number
# of sentences must match what the shots of a scene speak. A line "shown | spoken"
# shows the first part as the subtitle and speaks the second.
#
# Voice: Kokoro v1.0 (82M, Apache 2.0) through kokoro-onnx, voice af_heart at speed
# 0.95 (kokoro_say.py), the same voice as meerora's demo video.
#
# Pronunciation. Kokoro speaks the words of [pronounce] as the phonemes given there
# (espeak style IPA, en-us) and the rest of a sentence through espeak-ng.
#
#   Written        Said as                  Phonemes     Why
#   meertex        MEER-tek, like TeX       mˈɪɹtɛk      espeak says MEER-teks
#   LaTeX          LAY-tek                  lˈeɪtɛk      espeak spells it out, L A T E X
#   a LaTeX        uh LAY-tek               ɐ lˈeɪtɛk    "a" before a lexicon word came out as the letter A
#   Meerverse      MEER-verse, /ˈmɪɹvɝs/    mˈɪɹvɜːs     as in meerora's video
#   meertex.com    "meertex dot com"        (spoken form in the line below)
#
# The narration avoids written forms that read badly, such as AoE, CLI or latexdiff,
# which only appear on screen.

[pronounce]
meertex = mˈɪɹtɛk
LaTeX = lˈeɪtɛk
a LaTeX = ɐ lˈeɪtɛk
Meerverse = mˈɪɹvɜːs

[intro]
This is meertex, a LaTeX workspace for papers and the people who write them.
All your papers are in one place, each with its next deadline.

[local]
You write on your own computer, in your own editor or together with an LLM.
Every save reaches your coauthors within a second.

[browser]
Your coauthors edit the same document in the browser, and you see where they type.
Autocomplete knows your citations, and the PDF compiles as you write.

[refs]
Before you hand it in, meertex checks every reference against the publishers' records.
It catches wrong details and citations that do not exist.

[venue]
Every paper knows its venue and its deadlines, in the venue's time zone.
When a deadline passes, meertex asks what happened.
Say yes, and it keeps what you handed in as a fixed version.

[compare]
Compare any two versions, down to a PDF with every change marked.

[review]
Reviews work like pull requests on GitHub.
Reviewers select text in the PDF and comment on it right in the source.
When a comment is resolved, they see how the text was changed.

[timeline]
The timeline shows all your papers and where their writing time overlaps.

[agents]
Everything is there for agents too, through a command line tool and an API.
Ask an LLM what is due this month, or let it work through the open review comments.

[ending]
meertex. Write LaTeX locally. Your coauthors see it live.
meertex is part of the Meerverse.
Free and open source, at meertex.com. | Free and open source, at meertex dot com.
